Download images from Net

1. Open your preferred web browser (e.g., Google Chrome, Firefox, Edge).

2. Seach download free images in the address bar.

3. Open links in new tabs.

Note: More than one link are opened so that if you wouldn't get your desired image in the first website, you can search for it in the 2nd website and so on.

3. Open the first website.

4. Search your image in the search bar of the website.

5. You can also apply filters on the search results.

6. Click on any of the image of your choice.

7. Click the down arrow next to the download free button OR you can directly click on the download button also.

7. Select the required size.

8. Your picture has been downloaded. To check your downloaded image, click on the downloaded icon in the upper right corner of the screen.

9. If you recently downloaded the image, then it will shown in this mini-menu and you can open it from here.

10. Congratulations, your image has been downloaded and opened successfully.

Note: If you don't get the image of your choice in the 1st website, you can search it for in the another websites which we opened at the starting of this tutorial.
